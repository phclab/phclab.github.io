Classroom for Neuroscience

Interactive pages we use in class. Each one runs in the browser: change a parameter and see what the neuron, axon or circuit does.

Nernst–Goldman Explorer

Equilibrium potentials for K⁺, Na⁺, Cl⁻ and Ca²⁺, and how relative permeability sets the resting and peak membrane potential.

Open →

Myelin Cable Explorer

An axon as a chain of RC circuits: see how myelin changes how far and how fast a voltage step or pulse spreads.

Open →

Hodgkin–Huxley Model Explorer

Inject a current and watch m, h and n build an action potential: threshold, the refractory period and repetitive firing.

Open →

Neurotransmission

The five steps every synapse shares, how fast transmission differs from modulation, and ten transmitters in the same format.

Open →

Glutamate

The main excitatory transmitter: AMPA and NMDA receptors, coincidence detection and excitotoxicity.

GABA

The main inhibitory transmitter: chloride channels, benzodiazepines and why the chloride gradient decides the sign.

Dopamine

A modulator: four pathways, D1 versus D2, and reward prediction error.

DBS Explorer

Parkinson's disease, essential tremor and dystonia as live circuits. Place a deep brain stimulation electrode and see which mechanisms make it work.
