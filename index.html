Neuromodulation and Neuromorphic Computing

From understanding the brain to engineering the brain

We build wireless and non-invasive technologies for deep brain stimulation. Safe, low-power magnetic fields become precise bioelectric cues that reach deep circuits without bulky implants.

What we build

Four ways to reach a neuron

We combine neuroscience, nanotechnology, electrical and software engineering, molecular biology and animal studies, from the first nanoparticle to the freely moving mouse.

Wireless DBS

MagTIES

Torque-driven magnetoelectric nanostimulation that activates neurons within milliseconds and can set the rhythm of deep brain oscillations.

Read more →
Wireless DBS

Magnetomechanical stimulation

Magnetic nanodiscs that turn a weak alternating field into a mechanical push on mechanosensitive ion channels, with or without a transgene.

Read more →
Non-invasive

Temporal interference

Two high-frequency currents from the scalp that beat together only where they cross, so a deep target is stimulated and the surface is spared.

Read more →

Neuromorphic computing

Spiking networks wired like real brains and trained at scale, from the fruit-fly connectome to spiking language models.
